Drag a zoom to move it, its ends to change when it starts and stops, and the dots on top to change how long it takes to zoom in and out. In Framing, drag the box to choose where it zooms to, and its corners for how far – a pan or follow has a box for each framing it moves through, and a numbered mark on its block for when. Alt + scroll over the track zooms it in time; scroll then moves along it. Apply zooms puts them on the clip.

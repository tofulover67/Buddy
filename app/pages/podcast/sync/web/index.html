Podcast

1 The recordings

Every camera and recorder of one recording. Buddy groups the clips by the device they came from - each gets its own track - and keeps a camera's split files one after another.

2 Cameras and mics

Rename a device to name its track, put them in the order you want the tracks, and move a clip that landed in the wrong one. The master clock is what every other recording is measured against - usually the podcast's own recorder.

3 Sync

Sync by
Cameras' own sound
Syncing by sound needs ffmpeg, and Buddy couldn't find it. Install ffmpeg, or say where it is in Project Setup's settings.

You get a new timeline: a video track per camera and an audio track per mic. Nothing you already have is changed.

Canvas

Click a layer to change it. Shift-click picks more than one.

Number fields take sums (120+40, 300/2), and dragging a field's name changes it. Alt-drag a layer to copy it. Hold Alt while resizing to grow it from its middle, or over another layer to measure the gap.

Auto layout

Double-click to pick a layer inside. Drag a layer along the line to move it among the others, or off the frame to take it out.

Components

Pick some layers and click Create component (Ctrl+Alt+K) to make one.

Place puts an instance in the middle of the view. Instances follow their main component: change the main, and every instance changes with it. Duplicate or Alt-drag a main to make an instance too.

You get a new clip at the playhead. Nothing you already have is changed.
